Neat Trick or Treat / Halloween Guide (by Lunalia, thread created 29/09/25, last edited 19/12/25)

The Halloween event typically starts on October 25, with the exact time indicated in your calendar.

Earning Treats:
- You receive treats for defeating players.
- Defends don't earn treats.
- The base chance for receiving treats is 35% and increases by +5% with each basket upgrade, up to a max of 80%; and can increase by +20% when using scary outfit and weapon (non-stackable AND having unlocked the perks).
- On average, you receive 1.5 treats per attack. You can earn 1 to 120, or 1000 treats at once in the same attack, the odds of receiving more treats increase with better basket upgrades. For more details on the number of treats per attack, check this post (https://www.torn.com/forums.php#/p=threads&f=2&t=16296297&b=0&a=0).
- The level of the target, their stats, the outcome (leave, mug, hospitalize) don't impact the chances of receiving treats.

Obtaining a Basket:
Baskets become available to purchase starting at 12 TCT on the day the event starts. Purchase a basket from Sally's Sweet Shop for $35 (1k baskets are restocked every 5 minutes). You can still buy a basket after having met the 100 daily city shop purchase limit. The basket is a permanent item and upgrades carry over every year.

Halloween Basket Upgrades:
- Spooky: 0 treats, 0 total
- Creepy: 5 treats, 5 total
- Freaky: 10 treats, 15 total
- Frightful: 25 treats, 40 total
- Haunting: 50 treats, 90 total
- Shocking: 100 treats, 190 total
- Terrifying: 250 treats, 440 total
- Horrifying: 500 treats, 940 total
- Petrifying: 1000 treats, 1940 total
- Nightmarish: 2500 treats, 4440 total
- Apocalyptic: Only obtained by summoning M'aol
Note: The basket upgrades automatically after you've earned the number of treats listed.

Honors:
- Phantastic - Upgrade your Halloween Basket to Frightful
- Something Humerus - Upgrade your Halloween Basket to Terrifying
- Oh My Gourd! - Upgrade your Halloween Basket to Nightmarish

Important Note: Treats carry over every year. Don't click 'exchange all treats' at the end of the event till you've finished the other upgrades.

Finding Targets to Attack:
- Use FF Scouter to estimate if the target will be easy to defeat.
- Donator status gives access to Advanced Search, search for inactive players to defeat.
- Claim bounties

Obtaining Energy:
- Xanax
- Point Refills
- Energy Drinks
- Featherly Hotel Coupon
- Green Easter Eggs
- Seasonal Newsletter's +250 Energy
- Revitalize RW weapon (19%+ or lower percentages with better baskets). Check this post for more.
- Donator or subscriber status to increase energy regain
- Limit travel to not be hospitalized abroad (people will be attacking a lot!)
- Company Perks (Note: you can only exchange 100 job points max per day in energy providing perks - doesn't apply for E-drinks):
  - 7* Candle Shop: 5E for 1 jp
  - 10* Farm: 7E for 1 jp
  - 1* Furniture Store: 3E for 1jp
  - 10* Game Shop: 5E for 1 jp (plus 1 nerve and 50H)
  - 1* Pub: 3E for 1 jp
  - 1* Restaurant: 3E for 1 jp
  - 10* Grocery Store: 1 premium E-drink for 12 jp
  - 10* Sweet Shop: 1 premium E-drink for 15 jp
- Helpful Books:
  - Book : Higher Daddy, Higher! Provides = +20% energy regeneration for 31 days.
  - Book : Fuelling Your Way To Failure = Doubles energy drink effects for 31 days.
  - Book : Ugly Energy = Increases maximum energy (including energy refills) to 250 for 31 days (Pair this with FHCs!)

Why Participate?
Halloween is a very profitable event in the long run. After you have maxed the recommended upgrades, you are likely to make around $40m profits per 10 attacks (1 xanax). You also earn xp that contributes towards leveling up. Try to chain when attacking, which gives the faction respect!
